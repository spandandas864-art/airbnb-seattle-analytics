**Airbnb Seattle Business Analytics**

**Executive Summary**

The Seattle Airbnb portfolio consists of **3,730 total listings** with an estimated occupancy rate of **32.93%** and a daily average price of **$128.47** (median price of **$100.00**). Top revenue-generating neighborhoods include **Capitol Hill ($5.86M)** and **Belltown ($4.72M)**, driven primarily by higher listing volume. Guest capacity is the strongest indicator of listing price, showing a **65.27%** correlation. Additional findings indicate that strict cancellation policies command a significant price premium of **$45.30**, whereas Superhost status accounts for a modest price premium of only **$3.56 ($131.31 vs. $127.75)**.

**Strategic Business Recommendations**

1. Optimize Capacity Configuration: Prioritize expanding guest capacity. Because guest capacity has a strong 65.27% correlation with price, adding capacity (e.g., sofa beds, converting spaces) directly adds to higher rates.  
2.  Leverage Policy-Based Pricing: Shift inventory toward stricter cancellation policies where appropriate. Listings under strict policies yield a $45.30 average premium compared to flexible options, creating a key price generator for high-demand property types.   
3. Refine Superhost Value Proposition: Avoid relying solely on Superhost status to justify price premiums, as it yields only a $3.56 increase over standard listings. Focus host incentives on cleanliness, capacity, and location over status badges alone.   
4. Expand In High-Yield Neighborhoods: Focus growth and supply acquisition efforts in high-volume, high-revenue areas like Capitol Hill ($5.86M) and Belltown ($4.72M), while re-evaluating pricing strategies in lower-performing areas like the CBD ($1.28M).
